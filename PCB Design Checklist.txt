PCB mid-design checklist:

1 - Incorrect PCB traces:
	No sharp turns
	Traces carrying large currents should be wider than regular traces

2- Decoupling capacitors
	Bulk capacitoyrs must be next to the power pins
	
3- No length equalization of high-speed PCB traces
	All clock signal pins must be of the same length
	Trace Length Equalization
	Use delay lines to compensate for cases such as vias or constricted spacing

4- Improperly designed antenna feed lines
	Impedance matching: PCB trace with controlled impedance matching the antenna's
	Antennas must be clear of any ground planes (nothing paced around them)

5- Non-optimzed component placement
	Inductors shold not placed close to each other
	Inductors should not be placed closed to metallic objects
	Heat dissipation effects to consider for nearby components44

6- Incorrect design of grounds and ground planes
	Separate analog and digital ground planes

SilkScreen layer checklist:

	1- Polarity of parts
	2- Location of parts through reference designators
	3- Identify test points
	4- Identification numbers unique to each board
	5- Warning symbols


Users.Drawings layer checklist:

	1- Custom annotations
 	2- Graphical elements
	3- Dimensions and Measurements
	4- Masking and Overlay


User Comments layer:	documentation purposes -or- additional side information


Edge Cuts layer:	defining board outline, 
			mechanical dimensions, 
			keep-out areas to prevent interference
			panelization defining the boundaries between individual PCBs
			manufacturing constraints to guide the manufacturer on where to cut


Margin layer:	edge clearance, component clearance, keep-out areas, panelization margins
			ensuring enough space between adjacent PCBs


Courtyard layer:	area around a physical component
